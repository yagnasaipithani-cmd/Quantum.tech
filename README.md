# Quantum Cybersecurity Roadmap 2030 🚀

Welcome to the **Quantum Cybersecurity Roadmap**! Quantum computing is evolving rapidly, and whether it takes 4 years or 10 years, quantum computers are the future. Preparing now equips you with cutting-edge skills for the upcoming quantum era.

This repository provides a **100% Free** and structured learning path to master Quantum Cybersecurity and Quantum Computing from scratch.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

* **Objective:** A comprehensive, step-by-step roadmap to become a Quantum Cybersecurity Specialist by 2030.
* **Cost:** 100% Free Resources.
* **Target Audience:** Beginners, students, and tech enthusiasts ready to step into the future of computing.

---

## 📚 Curriculum Structure (5 Core Modules)

1. **Module 1: Foundations & Linux Basics**
   * Linux CLI fundamentals, shell scripting, and environment setup.
2. **Module 2: Mathematics & Physics Prerequisites**
   * Linear algebra, complex numbers, probability, and basic quantum mechanics.
3. **Module 3: Quantum Computing Basics**
   * Qubits, superposition, entanglement, and quantum gates.
4. **Module 4: IBM Qiskit & Hands-on Coding**
   * Building and running quantum circuits using IBM Qiskit on Google Colab.
5. **Module 5: Quantum Cryptography & Cybersecurity**
   * Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), Quantum Key Distribution (QKD), and security protocols.

---

## 🎯 Goal
To bridge the gap between current cybersecurity practices and quantum-safe protocols, helping learners build future-proof technical expertise.
